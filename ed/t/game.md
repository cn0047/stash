## GLI-19 - technical standards for Interactive Gaming Systems.

System - Interactive Gaming System.

1.3.2 This document should not be interpreted to mean that if the technology is not mentioned,
then it is not allowed.

2.2.2 Dates between all components that comprise the system shall be synchronized.

2.3.2 System shall be capable of verifying that all critical components
are authentic copies of the approved components.

2.4.1 System shall be able to disable: all gaming activity, individual game themes/paytables, individual player logins.

2.4.2 Changes to the parameters shall not affect the probabilities of triggering the current jackpot.

2.5.2 There shall be method to collect player’s personally identifiable information (PII)
prior to the registration of player account.

2.5.3 It's ok to have more than one method of authentication being available for player to access their account.
Passwords should be at least eight characters in length.

2.5.4 After thirty minutes of inactivity on that Remote Player Device, or a period determined by the regulatory body,
the player shall be required to re-authenticate to access their player account.
Once every thirty days, or a period specified by the regulatory body, the player will be required
to provide full authentication on that device.

2.5.6 The system shall provide confirmation/denial of every financial transaction initiated.
Funds shall not be available for wagering until they are received from the issuer or the issuer provides.
It shall not be possible to transfer funds between two player accounts.

2.5.7 The System shall be able to provide a transaction log or account statement
history to a player upon request.

2.5.8 Player loyalty programs - are any programs that provide incentive awards for players, typically
based on the volume of play or revenue received from a player.

2.6.2 Player Software shall contain sufficient information to identify the software and its version.

2.6.4 Player Software shall be designed or programmed such that it may only communicate with
authorized components through secure communications.
If communication between the System and the Remote Player Device is lost, the software shall prevent further gaming
operations and display an appropriate error message.

2.6.5 Player may obtain/download an application or software package containing the Player Software
or access the software via browser to take part in gaming and financial transactions with the System.
The software shall not contain any logic utilized to generate the result of any game.
All critical functions including the generation of any game outcome shall be generated by the Gaming Platform
and be independent of the Remote Player Device.

2.6.8 Where cookies are used, players shall be informed of the cookie use upon Player Software
installation or during player registration.

2.7.4 Where interactive gaming occurs over a public network, such as the internet,
System shall incorporate location detection service or application
to reasonably detect and dynamically monitor the location of player attempting to play game.

**2.8** Information to be maintained.

2.8.1 The system shall provide a mechanism to export the data for the purposes of data analysis
and auditing/verification (e.g., CSV, XLS).

2.8.2 For each individual game played, the information to be maintained and backed up by System shall include:
date and time, unique player ID,
denomination played for the game,
total amount wagered, including any incentive credits,
total amount won, any progressive jackpots or incrementing jackpots,
any non-wager purchase that occurred between the start of play and the end of play,
rake, commission, or fees collected,
the results of any player choices involved in the game outcome,
contributions to progressive jackpots or incrementing jackpots,
relevant location information,
the current status of the game (in progress, complete, interrupted, cancelled, etc.),
unique game cycle ID and/or gaming session ID,
and more (@see doc).

2.8.3 For each individual game theme and paytable available for play, the information to be maintained and backed up shall include:
date and time, unique player ID,
unique game theme/paytable ID, game configuration data,
the number of games played;
total value of all wagers, incentive credits wagered/won,
subsequent wagers of intermediate winnings,
total value paid as a result of winning wagers,
any incentive credits and/or prizes won, any progressive jackpots,
total double-up/gamble amount played/wagered/won,
total rake, commission, or fees collected,
the current status of the game theme/paytable (active, disabled, decommissioned, etc.),
and more (@see doc).

2.8.4 Contest/Tournament information to be maintained and backed up by the System shall include:
date and time, unique player ID,
name or identification of the contest/tournament,
participating game theme/paytable ID,
amount of entry fee collected, including any incentive credits,
player scorings/rankings, amount of winnings paid,
total rake, commission, or fees collected,
current status of the contest/tournament (in progress, complete, interrupted, cancelled, etc.),
and more (@see doc).

2.8.5 For each player account, the information to be maintained and backed up by the System shall include:
unique player ID and username, PII,
date and method of identity verification,
date of player agreement to the operator’s terms and conditions and privacy policy,
account details and current balance, including any incentive credits,
all restricted incentive credits and incentive credits that have a possible expiration shall be maintained separately,
previous accounts, if any, and reason for de-activation,
exclusions/limitations information as required by the regulatory body,
financial transaction information, persistence game information,
current status of the player account,
and more (@see doc).

2.8.6 For Systems which support incentive awards that are redeemable for cash, wagering, or merchandise,
the information to be maintained and backed up by the System shall include:
unique incentive offer ID, date and time the incentive was made available,
current balance, total amount issued/redeemed/expired, current status of the incentive,
and more (@see doc).

2.8.7 Jackpot information (@see doc).

2.8.8 Significant event information to be maintained and backed up by the System shall include:
significant periods of unavailability, large wins, system corrections, changes to incentive parameter,
and more (@see doc).

2.8.9 For each user account, the information to be maintained and backed up by the System shall include:
user identification,
date and time the account was created/disabled/last access/last password change, current status,
and more (@see doc).

**2.9** The System shall be capable of generating the information needed to compile reports as required by the regulatory body.

2.9.2 Game performance reports (@see doc).

2.9.3 Operator liability reports (@see doc).

2.9.4 Large jackpot payout reports (@see doc).

**3.1** Random Number Generator (RNG) requirements.

3.3.1 The RNG used in the determination of game outcomes in a Gaming Platform shall be cryptographically strong.

**4.1** Game requirements.

**4.2** Player Interface.

4.3.4 A player interface shall display the following information within a gaming session:
current funds available, denomination being played
current wager amount and placement of all active wagers,
and more (@see doc).

4.3.5 Depending on the implementation within the Gaming Platform,
funds may be transferred from the player account balance to a credit meter for the gaming session.
Once play is complete the player shall have the option to transfer some or all of their funds
back to their player account balance.
