## GLI-19 - technical standards for Interactive Gaming Systems.

System - Interactive Gaming System.

1.3.2 This document should not be interpreted to mean that if the technology is not mentioned,
then it is not allowed.

2.2.2 Dates between all components that comprise the system shall be synchronized.

2.3.2 System shall be capable of verifying that all critical components
are authentic copies of the approved components.

2.4.1 System shall be able to disable: all gaming activity, individual game themes/paytables, individual player logins.

2.4.2 Changes to the parameters shall not affect the probabilities of triggering the current jackpot.

2.5.2 There shall be method to collect player’s personally identifiable information (PII)
prior to the registration of player account.

2.5.3 It's ok to have more than one method of authentication being available for player to access their account.
Passwords should be at least eight characters in length.

2.5.4 After thirty minutes of inactivity on that Remote Player Device, or a period determined by the regulatory body,
the player shall be required to re-authenticate to access their player account.
Once every thirty days, or a period specified by the regulatory body, the player will be required
to provide full authentication on that device.

2.5.6 The system shall provide confirmation/denial of every financial transaction initiated.
Funds shall not be available for wagering until they are received from the issuer or the issuer provides.
It shall not be possible to transfer funds between two player accounts.

2.5.7 The System shall be able to provide a transaction log or account statement
history to a player upon request.

2.5.8 Player loyalty programs - are any programs that provide incentive awards for players, typically
based on the volume of play or revenue received from a player.

2.6.2 Player Software shall contain sufficient information to identify the software and its version.

2.6.4 Player Software shall be designed or programmed such that it may only communicate with
authorized components through secure communications.
If communication between the System and the Remote Player Device is lost, the software shall prevent further gaming
operations and display an appropriate error message.

2.6.5 Player may obtain/download an application or software package containing the Player Software
or access the software via browser to take part in gaming and financial transactions with the System.
The software shall not contain any logic utilized to generate the result of any game.
All critical functions including the generation of any game outcome shall be generated by the Gaming Platform
and be independent of the Remote Player Device.
